# Apex Global Technologies - Remote Work & Corporate Travel Policy
Document ID: AGT-POL-2026-004
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 3.2
Author: People Operations & Workplace Experience

1. Overview and Objective
This document outlines the corporate operating guidelines for flexible and remote working arrangements, as well as business travel expenditure standards for all employees of Apex Global Technologies.

2. Remote Work Eligibility and Guidelines
2.1 Eligibility:
Full-time permanent team members are eligible to request hybrid or fully remote working arrangements after successfully completing their 60-day probationary review period. Part-time contractors and temporary interns must obtain explicit approval from their Department Director.

2.2 Core Collaborative Hours:
To facilitate asynchronous and synchronous cross-functional collaboration, all remote personnel must maintain active presence and communication responsiveness between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST), Monday through Friday.

2.3 Equipment and Home Office Setup Allowance:
Apex Global Technologies provides a one-time home office equipment reimbursement of up to $1,200 (USD) to establish an ergonomic workspace. Eligible items include ergonomic chairs, external 4K monitors, standing desk converters, keyboards, and noise-canceling headsets. Receipts must be submitted through the Expensify portal within 45 days of hire.

2.4 Monthly Connectivity Stipend:
Remote employees receive a non-taxable monthly internet and cellular utility reimbursement of $60 (USD), disbursed automatically with the final monthly payroll cycle.

3. Corporate Travel and Expense Guidelines
3.1 Per Diem Meals & Incidentals:
When traveling on authorized company business exceeding 24 hours, employees receive a meal allowance capped at $75 (USD) per calendar day (Breakfast: $15, Lunch: $25, Dinner: $35). Alcoholic beverages are excluded from reimbursement.

3.2 Lodging and Accommodations:
Domestic hotel stays must be reserved via the corporate TravelPerk platform with a standard room rate cap of $220 (USD) per night excluding local municipal taxes. Reservations in Tier-1 metropolitan hubs (such as New York City, San Francisco, and London) permit an increased cap of $310 (USD) per night.

3.3 Ground Transportation:
Employees are encouraged to utilize licensed rideshare services (Uber/Lyft) or public transit. Rental cars require prior Vice President approval and are restricted to standard compact or mid-size categories.
