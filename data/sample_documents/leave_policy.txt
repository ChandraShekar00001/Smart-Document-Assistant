# Apex Global Technologies - Comprehensive Leave & Time-Off Policy
Document ID: AGT-POL-2026-009
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 4.1
Author: Total Rewards & HR Operations

1. Policy Purpose and Scope
Apex Global Technologies believes employee wellness, personal time, and work-life balance are critical to sustainable creativity and peak performance. This policy applies uniformly to all full-time salaried employees.

2. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
2.1 Entitlement:
Each full-time employee is entitled to 22 business days of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued semi-monthly at a rate of approximately 1.83 business days per calendar month.

2.2 Notice and Approval:
Requests for single-day leaves require a minimum of 48 hours advance notice via the HR portal. Continuous leaves exceeding 5 consecutive business days require two weeks prior written manager approval.

2.3 Year-End Rollover and Forfeiture:
A maximum of 5 accrued, unused PTO days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Any remaining accrued unused PTO balance beyond 5 days is strictly forfeited at 11:59 PM EST on December 31. Payout in cash for unused PTO is prohibited except upon official employment separation.

3. Sick and Medical Leave
3.1 Annual Sick Leave Allowance:
All employees receive 10 dedicated paid sick days on January 1 of each calendar year. Unused sick days do not accrue or carry over across calendar years.

3.2 Medical Documentation:
An official physician's medical certificate is mandatory for consecutive sick leave absences lasting 3 or more working days, to be submitted to HR Benefits within 5 business days of returning to work.

4. Parental and Family Caregiver Leave
4.1 Primary Caregiver Leave:
Eligible employees welcoming a new child through childbirth, adoption, or foster care placement receive 14 consecutive weeks of 100% company-paid primary caregiver leave.

4.2 Secondary Caregiver Leave:
Secondary caregivers are eligible for 6 consecutive weeks of 100% paid leave, which may be taken within the first 12 months following birth or placement.

5. Compassionate Bereavement Leave
Employees are granted up to 5 consecutive paid business days in the tragic event of the death of an immediate family member (defined strictly as legal spouse, registered domestic partner, child, parent, or sibling).
Extended travel beyond 500 miles allows an additional 2 unpaid bereavement days upon manager consent.
